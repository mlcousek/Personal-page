Jirka's World — my Obsidian vault

Everything in my life ends up in one place: an Obsidian vault I have been building since 2025. Training and racing, books, podcasts, money, work, trips — over 3 000 activities and thousands of notes, all linked together.

The rule is simple: data should arrive by itself. Scheduled Node.js scripts pull everything in, interactive DataviewJS dashboards turn it into answers, and a custom TypeScript plugin handles what Markdown can't — maps, charts and analysis, tested by thousands of regression checks.

The vault is also where my training plan lives: seasons split into phases, phases into weeks, weeks into sessions, with races anchored inside the phases. Every week is written together with Claude from my real data — recent volume, load, how I feel — and checked by a script against evidence-based planning rules before I approve it. It is the same plan my iPhone app Jirka's Arc shows: the app reads it from the vault, offers today's session, takes a morning check-in and writes back what really happened, while the vault stays the single source of truth.
